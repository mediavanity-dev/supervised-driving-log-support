Common questions

Who uses the app during a drive?

Only the supervising adult in the passenger seat. The driver never uses or touches the phone. While a drive is running the screen shows only the time and End drive. The app uses no GPS, motion tracking or notifications.

I forgot to start a drive. Can I add it?

Yes. Add it afterwards with its date and times. Drives added this way are marked "Entered later", so the log stays honest.

How are night minutes worked out?

From sunset and sunrise for your state, or from night hours you set yourself. Check what your state counts as night driving.

Will my state accept this log?

Requirements differ by state. This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by or approved by any state or licensing agency, and it is not an official log. Check what your state asks for and whether it accepts a summary like this.

How do I keep my records safe, or move them to a new phone?

Save a backup file from the app and keep it somewhere safe; on the new phone use Restore backup. You can also add drives logged on another parent's phone, and export everything to a spreadsheet (CSV), free.

What does Pro add, and how do I restore it?

Pro is a one-time purchase that adds more than one driver and a printable summary (PDF) of every drive with totals. To restore it on a new iPhone, open the Pro page in the app and tap Restore, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.
