Privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Supervised Driving Log collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app makes no network requests of its own.

Your log

Drivers, drives, supervisors, conditions and notes are stored on your phone only. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. If you back up your phone (iCloud or a computer), they are included in that backup under your own account.

No location

The app does not use GPS or location services. Night minutes come from sunset and sunrise tables for the state you choose.

Files you share

Backups, spreadsheets and PDF summaries are created on your phone and go only where you choose to send them through the share sheet.

Purchases

The optional Pro purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, your payment details or your Apple Account.

Email

If you email support, your address and message are used only to reply to you.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
